# Design

This folder leads the lab. A change starts here and the scripts follow it.

## Files

- `ironcore-in-a-box-fit.md`: what ironcore-in-a-box gives a librbd prototype
  and what is missing, checked against its code. It says what the lab has to
  add.
- `design-decisions.md`: why the lab is built the way it is. Decisions are
  numbered and never deleted or renumbered. A revised one is marked
  "Superseded by". Deferred work and open questions are under TODO in that
  file.
- `development.md`: where things live, the order of work with a "Where we
  are" checklist, and the record of what was run and measured.
